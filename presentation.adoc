== One Path to Technologist

// GOAL: introduce myself

[.notes]
--
* I say _we_ because like most of you, I consider myself to be a technologist.
* I studied electrical engineering in college, and quickly found myself interested in building software for space systems.
* _Space is cool!_
* Thanks to a lot of privilege and luck, and thanks to my ability to apply Fourier transforms, I was accepted into a rotational internship program at NASA, and ultimately hired as a full-time integrated systems engineer .
* (This picture is me, one pandemic and a couple years younger, sitting on a space toilet on a tour.)
* Today, I work as a systems software engineer at the Robotics & AI Institute near Boston; I build tools for interacting with robot hardware.

== Definition of Technologist

[.notes]
--
* Over time, I've grown to really love the term _technologist_ because it get's to *what* we do much more effectively than _engineer_.
* For the purposes of this presentation, a technologist is someone who does at least one of two things: helps to develop technology, or helps to spread the use of technology.
* I include scientists, engineers, researchers, people operations departments, and more.
--

TECHNOLOGIST:: A person who contributes to the development or proliferation of emerging technologies.
