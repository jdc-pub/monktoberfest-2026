= What Technologists Owe Creatives
Joey Carpinelli <joseph.carpinelli@icloud.com>

If you had listened to my wife's MFA cohort throughout the duration of her program, you might have have thought Laramie, Wyoming to be among the more magical places on earth.
Within the first few weeks of her first semester, she learned from the more senior class that all residents of Laramie have an _enemy_.
Someone else in town --- a cashier, a colleague, perhaps a stranger met randomly in the street --- will be revealed to be in total opposition to you at some point in your stay.

Later in her first semester, I received a frantic phone call: she had gone for a walk off campus, and crossed paths with a small child playing with a lightsaber on the sidewalk.
She smiled and asked: "excuse me, could I please walk by?"
He stood up, screamed --- "nooo!" --- and began to whack her multiple times, chasing her as she put up her hands and hastened past.
